### Understanding the settings

- **Aperture** affects depth of field and the amount of light reaching the sensor.
- **Shutter speed** determines exposure duration and how movement is recorded.
- **ISO** describes the camera's sensitivity setting.

### About this information

The camera settings shown in the viewer come from the gallery metadata. They are
not extracted from the compressed display image. Longer notes can describe
lighting, equipment, composition, or editing without covering the photograph.

Read more about [EXIF metadata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exif).
